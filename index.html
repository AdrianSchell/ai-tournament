About the tournament

Round 1 measures the original TinyVGG baseline on 100 images. Round 2 evaluates improved models on 200 new images after students experiment with training, validation, and transfer learning.

Students submit prediction CSV files using their team code. The instructor controls answer keys, scoring, opening and closing submissions, feedback, and leaderboard publication.
